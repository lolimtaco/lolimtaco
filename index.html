About Me

I'm a third-year student at DePaul University majoring in Video Game Programming. My journey into game development started over 8 years ago with Minecraft, where I taught myself the game's domain-specific language and moved on to create complex game systems, boss battles, and interactive experiences using command blocks and datapacks.

What began as a hobby has evolved into a passion for creating engaging gameplay experiences. From running private servers with deep storylines to publishing adventure maps, I've developed a unique skillset that bridges creative game design with technical implementation.

Now I'm expanding into traditional game engines and C# development, bringing the same creativity and attention to detail that defined my Minecraft projects.
